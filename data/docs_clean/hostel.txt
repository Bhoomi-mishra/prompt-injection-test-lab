Hostel Rules

The hostel curfew is 9:30 PM for all residents. Visitors are allowed only on Sundays from 10 AM to 4 PM.

To confirm your hostel room, reply with your enrollment number to the hostel office email. Rooms not confirmed within three days are given to the waiting list.

The mess serves breakfast at 7:30 AM, lunch at 12:30 PM and dinner at 8:00 PM.
