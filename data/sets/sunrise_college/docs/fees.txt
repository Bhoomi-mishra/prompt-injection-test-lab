Sunrise College Fee Structure

The annual tuition fee for the B.Tech programme is Rs. 85,000. The fee must be paid before 10 July each year.

The hostel fee is Rs. 60,000 per year, which includes mess charges and laundry.

A late fee of Rs. 200 per day is charged for payments made after the due date. The examination fee is Rs. 1,500 per semester.
